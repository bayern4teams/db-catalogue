## A1/D2/D9: REJECT (two small fixes, one risk to record)

Holds: boundary at the configured threshold for support 1, 2, default, default+3 (side below it keeps the candidate marked "sample too small", at it and above it drops; the smaller side decides, so asymmetric 500 vs N-1 keeps and 500 vs N drops); 1 shared of 500 never drops at any threshold; a zero-distinct child side is "not measured", not a counterexample; key-ID mismatch labelled and kept; composite candidates untouched by disjoint tokens; declared FK never dropped; config rejects 0, -1, "many", 2.5, an unknown sibling key (`extra=forbid`), loads 7 from TOML and names the field on a bad value; run field `dropped_zero_overlap_candidates` = 2 for two drops and absent when 0 or sampling off; a dropped candidate leaves no edge and no `name_similarity`/`compatible_type`/key/overlap evidence; every remaining evidence row is cited; edge and evidence ids identical across two builds; the unmeasured and measured evidence for one pair never coexist; no digest, sampled value, or reason text reaches any snapshot file, the rendered page, logs (DEBUG) or stdout/stderr.

Defects (each has a failing test):
1. **Drop on a truncated parent sample is unsound.** `test_RISK_a_truncated_parent_set_does_not_prove_the_child_is_not_contained`: the collector keeps the lowest-hash `cap` digests when a field has more distinct values than the cap; a child whose 8 values are all in the parent's full sample shares none of the kept subset (cap 40 of 2000: probability ~0.85) and is dropped as "measured 0 of 8". The code's own docstring says only a zero *measurement* may drop, and the metric already carries `lower_bound_only`, but the drop ignores it. Same family: `test_RISK_an_empty_kept_token_set_is_not_zero_overlap` (parent with distinct 50 but no kept tokens is dropped). Fix: do not drop (keep, label lower bound) when the parent set is truncated or empty; a truncated child with zero shared is still sound (characterised, passes). Note a truncated child can still lose a ~1% real overlap (zero of 40 kept), which the user's "non-zero never drops" is about the full data; record as a known limit.
2. **Drop after `top_k_per_field` hides the true parent.** `test_a_dropped_top_candidate_does_not_hide_a_lower_ranked_true_one`: with `top_k_per_field=1` the best name match (disjoint) is dropped after the slice and the second (overlapping) parent was never tried; with `top_k_per_field=5` it is found (control in the same test). Fix: apply the slice after the overlap check.
